Meet Your Host

Dishank Timble

Filmmaker & Host of Kissey Podcast

Dishank Timble is a Mumbai-based filmmaker and the host of the Kissey Podcast. With a background in mass media and professional cinematography, he uses his visual and conversational expertise to bring deep, taboo-breaking dialogues to light. His purpose is simple: to bridge the gap between ancient wisdom and modern realities, providing Gen-Z and millennial audiences with an authentic space to explore the complex layers of human life.
