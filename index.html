What is Kissey?

Every Story Has a Side. Every Side Deserves a Voice.

Kissey is more than a podcast. It is a storytelling and conversation platform dedicated to exploring the realities of human life.

From unheard personal experiences and taboo subjects to spirituality, relationships, psychology, society and extraordinary human journeys — Kissey brings forward conversations that are often ignored, misunderstood or left unspoken.

We believe that behind every individual is a story. Behind every decision is an experience. And behind every experience is a perspective waiting to be understood.

We don't exist to tell people what to think. We exist to give them something worth thinking about.

Through long-form conversations, real-life stories, powerful interviews and thought-provoking content, Kissey aims to challenge perspectives, encourage curiosity and create meaningful connections.

Letters With Kissey.

Your Story. Your Truth. Your Voice.

Some stories are never told. Some feelings are never expressed. Some experiences stay buried because the world doesn't always make it easy to speak.

Letters With Kissey is a space created for those stories.

It is a platform where people can share their personal experiences, anonymous confessions, secrets, heartbreaks, relationships, struggles, unusual encounters and moments that changed their lives.

Whether it's something you've never told anyone, a memory you cannot forget, a truth you've been carrying for years or simply a story you want the world to hear — this is your space.

You can write under your name or remain anonymous. There is no requirement to be extraordinary, famous or influential. You just have to be real.

Because sometimes, writing down what you've been holding inside is the first step towards letting it go. And sometimes, your story might be exactly what someone else needs to read.

Meet the Founder

Dishank Timble

Founder & Host, Kissey Podcast

Dishank Timble is a storyteller, content creator and the founder of Kissey Podcast — a platform built around one simple belief: every person has a story worth hearing.

With a passion for human psychology, untold experiences, spirituality, relationships and the complexities of human life, Dishank created Kissey to bring conversations out of the shadows and into the open.

His vision goes beyond entertainment. It is about questioning perspectives, exploring uncomfortable truths, breaking social barriers and creating a space where people can speak without the fear of being judged.

Through Kissey, Dishank aims to build a community where stories don't just get heard — they make people think.
